# V2 content audit

This audit treats `src/content/portfolio.ts` as the current verified register.
It tightens presentation copy without manufacturing biography, outcomes,
dates, users, awards, publication status, or impact.

## Homepage copy sheet

### HEARTLINE

- What it is: a native Android lyric companion for active playback,
  synchronized lyrics, offline access, live notifications, and on-device lyric
  cards.
- Role: independent product engineering.
- Human sentence: **Lyrics that stay with the song—even when the connection
  does not.**
- Strongest proof: native Android; playback-aware; offline cache.
- Action: view the case study. The public GitHub repository is the secondary
  action inside the case study.
- Evidence required: capture a cleared, coherent phone-screen sequence using
  rights-safe demonstration audio and lyric content.
- Private/copyright boundary: no uncleared lyrics, album artwork, artist
  metadata, listening history, or personal device data.

### CancerShift

- What it is: an independent computational-biology study testing whether a
  multicancer classifier transfers across studies, platforms, and cohorts.
- Role: independent computational-biology research.
- Human sentence: **A classifier is only convincing after the dataset
  changes.**
- Strongest proof: 99.59% blind external microarray accuracy; 98.24% external
  RNA-seq accuracy; explicit leakage control.
- Action: read the research case study.
- Evidence required: remake a publication-safe validation map and result figure
  from approved aggregate values; verify every cohort label before publication.
- Private/unpublished boundary: no unverified accessions, sample counts,
  selective-accuracy claims, original figures, or clinical-utility implication.
  The work is not represented as peer reviewed.

### To Ash Again

  characters and six abilities.
- Role: independent game development.
- Human sentence: **A four-act story built out of character state, powers,
  enemies, saves, and consequences.**
- Strongest proof: four acts; two characters; six abilities.
- Action: enter the case study.

### Flooded

## Copy to delete or rewrite

Delete language whose subject is the portfolio rather than the work:

- explanations of “worlds,” “departments,” “tracks,” “issues,” or transitions;
- repeated claims that a project makes something “coherent,” “understandable,”
  “alive,” or “navigable” without concrete evidence;
- publication-boundary paragraphs in the primary narrative.

Publication boundaries remain visible as concise disclosure notes and in the
asset register. They should not become the emotional center of every case
study.
