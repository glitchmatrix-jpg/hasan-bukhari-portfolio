# V2 asset recovery and publication register

Status vocabulary:

- **Approved** — may ship with source and rights recorded.
- **Candidate** — authentic and located, but needs selection or provenance
  review.
- **Capture** — authentic source exists; a new public-safe screenshot/render is
  required.
- **Blocked** — cannot ship until permission, privacy, or publication review is
  complete.
- **Missing** — described previously but not present in the current workspace.

## Publication checklist for every selected asset

- creator and copyright owner;
- original source URL or local source path;
- license and required attribution;
- commercial-use and modification permissions;
- acquisition date;
- edits performed;
- people/identity consent where relevant;
- sensitive-data and private-notes review;
- accessibility description or alt decision;
- optimized derivative filename and dimensions;
- final publication approval.

No Pinterest, portfolio screenshot, album art, lyric text, paper scan, font,
texture, model, or photo enters production without this record.
