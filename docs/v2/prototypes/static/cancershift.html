01 / The question

Does it survive the shift?

Does a cancer classifier still work when the study, sequencing platform, and patient cohort change?

CancerShift treats leakage control and external evaluation as the study, not as a final decorative benchmark.

Evaluation structure / publication-safe remake Development ≠ external

02 / The finding

Two strong external results.

The approved public record supports 99.59% accuracy on a blind external microarray cohort and 98.24% accuracy on external RNA-seq data.

The composition keeps the result attached to its evaluation setting instead of turning both numbers into floating claims.

03 / Limitations

Accuracy is not clinical utility.

  • The project is independent and not represented as peer reviewed.
  • The approved metrics do not establish clinical utility.
  • Unverified accessions, sample counts, and original figures stay out.
